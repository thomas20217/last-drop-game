BATTLE ROYALE

Drop into a shrinking arena, scavenge supplies, and outplay 32 skill-based bots. Bots use cover, aim, flank, retreat, loot, and fight each other. Be the last one standing.

Desktop: Player 1 moves with WASD, aims with mouse and fires with click. Player 2 moves with arrow keys, aims with I/J/K/L and fires Enter. On touchscreens, drag the left stick to move and hold FIRE to shoot; use the player count selector for two players. Pick up glowing loot by walking over it. Stay inside the safe circle!
